```
Saqqar is an AI-powered operations copilot for Odoo. Ask in natural Arabic or English, get answers, run reports, and act on your live ERP — all from a single secure conversation.

WHAT SAQQAR DOES
• Query Odoo in plain language — ask about customers, invoices, users, inventory, employees, anything in your database, and Saqqar reads from your live data and answers immediately.
• Generate reports — export filtered Odoo data to Excel, PDF, or Word in seconds. The agent picks the right fields, formats the file, and delivers it ready to share.
• Multi-environment workflows — switch between staging and production with one tap. Live environments require explicit confirmation before any message is sent.
• Approval gating — destructive actions (writes, deletes, file generation) require your explicit in-app approval. Nothing touches live data without your sign-off.
• Bilingual UI — full Arabic and English interface, full RTL support, with localized date and number formatting.
• Built for teams — manage multiple projects, each with its own environments and credentials. Switch contexts without losing chat history.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign in with your Saqqar account (saqqaar.com).
2. Add Odoo credentials securely — they're encrypted on-device in the Android Keystore.
3. Pick a project and environment.
4. Chat naturally. Saqqar reads your Odoo data when needed, asks for approval before acting, and explains every step.

WHY SAQQAR
• Built specifically for Odoo — every tool understands Odoo's models, fields, and relationships, so the agent's answers are grounded in your real ERP, not a generic LLM guess.
• Designed for operators, not engineers — no SQL, no API docs, no XML-RPC clients. If you can describe what you want, Saqqar can do it.
• Security-first — credentials never appear in logs, never in screenshots, and never sync to cloud services beyond the Saqqar backend you've authorized.
• Works on the go — Android-optimized chat surface tested on screens from 320 px (small Android phones) up to 7" tablets.

SUPPORT

Website: https://digitalharbor.com.sa

PRIVACY
Your conversations and Odoo data stay within your Saqqar account. We never sell or share your data. Full policy: https://digitalharbor.com.sa/privacy-saqqar

Made by Digital Harbor in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
```

## Notes for the uploader

1. **App name** must match what shows on the device launcher. If the
   Android label is "Saqqar" but the listing says "Saqqar — Odoo
   Copilot", that's fine — Play allows a longer marketing name.
2. **Don't put the company name** at the start of the app name — Play
   considers it a ranking penalty in search.
3. **First two lines** of the full description are what shows above
   the fold. Make them count.
4. **Tags** appear in search and in "Similar apps" suggestions —
   pick wisely. Don't add irrelevant tags hoping for more traffic;
   Google demotes apps with mismatched tags.
